PRIVACY POLICY

How we handle your information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Wanderly Travels collects, uses, stores and protects information provided by visitors, customers and people who contact us about travel services.

A simple promise from Wanderly Travels

We aim to collect only the information needed to provide better travel planning, enquiry and support services.

01 Information We Collect

When you contact us, enquire about a package, request a customized trip or otherwise use our services, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide.

  • Name and contact details such as email address and phone number.
  • Travel preferences, destination interests and trip requirements.
  • Booking-related details needed to organize your requested services.
  • Information you submit through forms, enquiries or other communication channels.

We may also collect limited technical information, such as browser type, device information and pages visited, when you interact with our website.

02 How We Use Your Information

Information you provide helps us respond to enquiries and deliver a smoother travel-planning experience.

  • To respond to your enquiries and requests.
  • To prepare travel packages and customized itineraries.
  • To communicate important information regarding your enquiry or booking.
  • To improve our website, services and customer experience.
  • To understand general website usage and improve our content.

03 Information Sharing

Wanderly Travels does not intend to sell your personal information to third parties.

Where necessary to provide a requested travel service, relevant information may need to be shared with trusted service providers such as hotels, transportation partners, activity providers or other suppliers involved in your itinerary.

We aim to share only information that is reasonably necessary for the requested service or communication.

04 Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support basic website functionality, understand visitor behaviour and improve the browsing experience.

Cookies are small files stored on your device by a website. Depending on your browser and settings, you may be able to control or disable cookies through your browser preferences.

05 Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information shared with us against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

06 Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, managing bookings and meeting applicable administrative or legal requirements.

07 Your Choices & Rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have choices regarding the personal information we hold about you.

  • Ask what information we may hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to stop certain non-essential communications.
  • Request deletion of information where applicable.

To make a request, please contact us using the details provided below.

08 Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites or services operated by third parties. These websites have their own privacy policies and practices.

Wanderly Travels is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before submitting personal information.

09 Children's Privacy

Our website and travel services are intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children without appropriate involvement from a parent or guardian.

10 Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website practices or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
